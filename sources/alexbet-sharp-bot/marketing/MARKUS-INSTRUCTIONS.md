## 🎯 ENGAGEMENT RULES (Non-Negotiable)

### Reply to EVERY Comment

**Why:** X algorithm boosts posts with high engagement in the first 30 minutes.

**How:**
- Turn on notifications for your own tweets
- When someone comments, reply within 5 minutes if possible
- Don't just say "thanks" — add value:
  - Bad: "Thanks!"
  - Good: "Appreciate that. What sport are you scanning most?"
  - Good: "Exactly. The math doesn't lie. What edge size do you usually target?"

### Quote Tweet Strategy

**Goal:** Get in front of other people's audiences.

**How:**
- Find 3-5 betting-related tweets per day
- Quote tweet them with INSIGHT, not just agreement
- Example:
  - Original tweet: "Lakers are +6.5 tonight, seems like easy money"
  - Your quote tweet: "+6.5 at -110 implies 52.4% win rate. If you think they cover 58%, that's +5.6% edge. If not, it's a pass. The number matters more than the team."

### DM Strategy

**Cold DMs (5 per day):**
- Find betting cappers/analysts with 10k-50k followers
- Send: "Love your content on [specific topic]. I built a +EV scanner — free tier if you want to test it. No strings."
- Track responses in a spreadsheet

**Warm DMs (5 per day):**
- People who liked/retweeted your posts
- People who commented thoughtfully
- Send: "Appreciate the engagement today. What sport are you focusing on?"
- If they reply, offer the bot link
