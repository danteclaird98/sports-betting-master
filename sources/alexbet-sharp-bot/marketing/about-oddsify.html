Our Story

Oddsify Labs was founded by Collins & Collins Technologies, a family-run technology holding company with a simple mission: build tools that give everyday people an edge.

We don't sell picks. We don't have a "lock of the week." What we do is build software that finds +EV (positive expected value) opportunities in sports betting markets — the same way hedge funds find alpha in financial markets.

Every product we ship is built on the Hermes Agent Engine, our proprietary multi-agent AI system. That means our software is coded, designed, written, and marketed by a team of specialized AI agents working together — each with a distinct skill set and personality.

Why agents? Because one generalist AI can't outwork a team of specialists. And when those specialists never sleep, never miss a detail, and iterate in real-time, you get products that move faster than anything else in the space.
