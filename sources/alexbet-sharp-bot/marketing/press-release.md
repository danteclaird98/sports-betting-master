## Body

**QUEEN CREEK, Arizona** — Oddsify Labs, a sports betting technology company and subsidiary of Collins & Collins Technologies, today announced the launch of **AlexBET Sharp**, a freemium Telegram bot that uses artificial intelligence to find profitable edges in sports betting markets.

But the product itself isn't the only story. AlexBET Sharp is believed to be one of the first commercially available SaaS products built almost entirely by a team of autonomous AI agents — a system Oddsify Labs calls the **Hermes Agent Engine**.

"Most people are using AI to write emails," said Jesse J. Collins, CTO and Operations Manager at Oddsify Labs. "We're using AI to build entire products. Miah codes the backend. Ruth builds the frontend. Octavia writes the copy. Markus runs social. Mitch handles marketing strategy. And they all coordinate through the Hermes engine. It's not one AI doing everything — it's a team of specialists, just like a real startup."

### What AlexBET Sharp Does

The bot analyzes real-time odds across six sports — NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, ATP Tennis, and EPL Soccer — and identifies +EV (positive expected value) opportunities. Unlike traditional handicappers who sell picks based on intuition, AlexBET Sharp surfaces data-driven edges backed by:

- **Implied vs. True Probability Calculations** — powered by Anthropic's Claude AI
- **Kelly Criterion Bankroll Sizing** — automatic stake recommendations customized to each user's bankroll
- **Closing Line Value (CLV) Tracking** — proof that users are consistently beating the market
- **Data Export** — CSV, JSON, and PDF downloads for users building their own models

The free tier offers 5 scans per day. Premium tiers — Sharp ($49/month) and Elite ($99/month) — unlock AI analysis, player props, unlimited scans, and API access.

"People think AI replaces humans," said Parris Collins, CEO and CFO of Oddsify Labs. "We think AI replaces the bottlenecks. The Hermes engine lets us move at startup speed with a fraction of the headcount. That means better products, lower prices, and faster iteration for our users."
