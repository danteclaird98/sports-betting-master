Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before you start.

Is this legal? +
Yes. AlexBET Sharp is an analytics and data tool. We don't place bets for you. We scan public odds data, run mathematical analysis, and present +EV opportunities. You decide what to bet and where. Always check your local regulations.
How is this different from a tout / picks service? +
Touts sell you their opinion. We sell you the TOOL that finds edges. You see the math — implied probability, true probability, edge percentage, and Kelly sizing. No "locks of the century." Just data-driven +EV plays you can verify yourself.
What sportsbooks do you scan? +
We pull odds data from major markets via The Odds API. The bot shows you the edge — you shop for the best line at your preferred sportsbook (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, etc.). Line shopping is part of the edge.
Can I cancel anytime? +

Do you guarantee wins? +
No. No one can guarantee wins in sports betting. What we guarantee is this: every play we surface has positive expected value. Over a large sample size, +EV betting wins. But variance is real — bankroll management matters.
